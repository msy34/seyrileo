## Owner steps (App Store Connect)

1. **Bundle ID** — `com.msy.seyrileo` (set 2026-10-25). Installs of the old placeholder ID are a
   separate app; sources must be added again in the new one.
2. **Create the app** in App Store Connect (My Apps → +): name "Seyrileo" (must be free on the
   store), primary language English (U.S.), the bundle ID, SKU of your choice.
3. **Privacy policy URL and support URL** — publish `docs/appstore/site/` (e.g. GitHub Pages
   from a public repository) after filling in the support e-mail.
4. **App Privacy** questionnaire: "Data Not Collected".
5. **Age rating** questionnaire (see `age_rating_note` in metadata.json).
6. **Pricing**: Free, all territories you want. No Paid Applications agreement is needed for a
   free app without purchases.
7. **VLCKit (LGPL-2.1):** owner accepted the risk on 2026-10-25 with Claude's mitigations (below);
   a lawyer's review is still recommended. For **every** uploaded build run
   `scripts/make-lgpl-relink-kit.sh` and keep `build/lgpl-relink-kit-<version>-<build>.zip` for at
   least three years (e.g. as an asset of a private GitHub release); `build/` is not in git.
8. **Rights / trademark** check for the name and icon.

## LGPL-2.1 measures for MobileVLCKit (done 2026-10-25)

- Unmodified official VideoLAN binary, dynamically linked (`Frameworks/MobileVLCKit.framework`).
- Notice, source link and full licence text: Settings → Open-source licenses.
- Written offer (§6c), in the app (7 languages) and on the support page: VLCKit source plus the
  object files to relink Seyrileo, on request, for at least three years; users may modify the app
  for their own use and reverse engineer it to debug such modifications (§6 terms).
- Relink kit per build: `scripts/make-lgpl-relink-kit.sh` (object files, linker command,
  `relink.sh`). Verified: unpacked in a fresh folder, `relink.sh` produced an arm64 Seyrileo
  binary linked to `@rpath/MobileVLCKit.framework`.
- Remaining risk (not legal advice): a copyright holder could still object to App Store terms and
  ask Apple to remove the app (as with VLC itself in 2011, before libVLC moved to LGPL).

## Upload and submit (after step 1–3)

1. `xcodebuild archive -project Seyrileo.xcodeproj -scheme Seyrileo -configuration Release
   -destination 'generic/platform=iOS' -archivePath build/Seyrileo.xcarchive -allowProvisioningUpdates`
2. Upload: Xcode → Window → Organizer → Distribute App → App Store Connect (signed in with the
   owner's Apple ID), or `xcodebuild -exportArchive` with an App Store export options plist.
3. TestFlight internal testing first (owner's iPhone and iPad): PiP, AirPlay, reminders,
   parental PIN with Face ID, all languages, iPad layout.
4. In App Store Connect: paste text from `metadata.json`, upload screenshots, add review notes,
   choose the build, **Submit for Review** (owner).
