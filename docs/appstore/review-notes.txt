Seyrileo is a media player for Xtream-compatible IPTV services that the user already subscribes to. The app does not include, sell, recommend or link to any channels, films or series, and it has no in-app purchases.

How to review without an account:
1. On first launch tap "Try the sample experience".
2. The app starts a small fictional catalogue that is served from the device itself (127.0.0.1): live channels with a TV guide, films and series. All names, artwork and video were generated by the developer; there is no third-party content.
3. Things to try: play a live channel, minimise the player (chevron) and browse, Picture in Picture, TV guide (calendar icon in Live), films and series in Library, Settings > Parental controls.

Users add their own source on the "Add your source" screen (server address, user name, password). Credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain and are only sent to that server. There is no account, analytics or tracking.

Network: users' providers often serve plain HTTP and redirect media to changing hosts and IP addresses, so the app sets NSAllowsArbitraryLoads. Certificate validation is never disabled and HTTPS sources are never downgraded.

Background audio is used so playback continues in Picture in Picture and with the screen locked.

Open-source components and licences are listed in Settings > Open-source licenses (MobileVLCKit, LGPL-2.1, dynamically linked and unmodified; GRDB, MIT).
