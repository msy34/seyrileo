Privacy Policy

Seyrileo for iPhone and iPad · Last updated 25 October 2026

Seyrileo is a media player. It does not collect, sell or share personal data, and it has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • The provider sources you add (server address and user name) and your settings, favorites, collections, watch progress and reminders are stored only on your device.
  • Passwords and the parental-controls PIN are stored in the iOS Keychain. The PIN is kept only as a one-way hash.
  • Deleting the app, or Settings → Remove all data, removes this information.

Connections the app makes

Seyrileo connects only to the provider servers you add yourself, to load their lists, programme guide, artwork and streams. What those servers receive and keep is governed by your provider's own policy. Seyrileo's developer has no server and receives nothing.

Diagnostics

If you choose to share a diagnostics report, you decide where to send it. Reports are redacted: they never contain passwords or stream addresses.

Notifications, Face ID and Touch ID

Programme reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Face ID / Touch ID is handled by iOS; Seyrileo never receives biometric data.

Children

Seyrileo does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: github.com/msy34/seyrileo/issues.

Gizlilik Politikası

Seyrileo bir medya oynatıcıdır. Kişisel veri toplamaz, satmaz, paylaşmaz; hesap, reklam, analiz ve izleme yoktur.

Eklediğiniz kaynaklar (sunucu adresi, kullanıcı adı), ayarlarınız, favorileriniz, koleksiyonlarınız, izleme ilerlemeniz ve hatırlatıcılarınız yalnızca cihazınızda saklanır. Parolalar ve ebeveyn denetimi PIN'i iOS Anahtar Zinciri'ndedir; PIN yalnızca tek yönlü özet olarak tutulur.

Seyrileo yalnızca sizin eklediğiniz sağlayıcı sunucularına bağlanır. Geliştiricinin sunucusu yoktur ve hiçbir veri almaz. Tanılama raporunu yalnızca siz paylaşırsınız; raporlarda parola ve yayın adresi bulunmaz.
